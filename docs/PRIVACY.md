# HydroNudge privacy policy — draft

Effective date, publisher name and support contact: supply before publication.

HydroNudge is a free, offline fluid intake tracker. It has no accounts, advertising, subscriptions, analytics, tracking SDKs, or Internet permission. The publisher does not receive your hydration records or device identifiers.

Drink names, volumes, categories, timestamps, notes, favorites and preferences are stored locally in the app’s private storage. They are used to display progress, history and optional notifications and widgets. Notification content may be visible on the lock screen according to your Android settings. Android cloud backup is disabled.

You can remove drink history in Settings or erase all app data in Android Settings. Uninstalling removes the app’s private data. Files you explicitly export are outside the app’s control; keep them private and delete them separately. A document provider you choose may itself use cloud storage.

Notifications are optional and can be disabled in the app or Android settings. The notification permission is requested only when you enable notification features. HydroNudge does not request location, contacts, microphone, accessibility, overlay or exact-alarm access.

The app is a fluid-volume journal, not a medical device, and does not provide personalized medical advice. The starting goal is editable. Speak to an appropriate health professional about individual fluid limits or needs.

Before release, reconcile this policy with shipped features, add a support contact and publish it at a publicly accessible URL.
